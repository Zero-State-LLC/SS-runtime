# VFX pool

Read 2026-11-04 from `VFXPool` on `5d954be`. No lifetime was changed.

Hit-stop frames and the 16-emitter ceiling stay in [hit-stop-2026-11-04.md](hit-stop-2026-11-04.md). Shake stays in [camera-follow-2026-11-03.md](camera-follow-2026-11-03.md).

`VFXRenderer` admits a tick's presentations at the current unfrozen frame. A recipe's live count stays at its `poolSize`, or 1 when the catalog has no size for it. At the limit, the instance with the lowest `sequence` leaves. After that pass, while the live count is above `maxConcurrentEmitters`, the pool removes the highest rank, and the lowest `sequence` among that rank. A presentation removed in the same call is neither admitted nor reported as evicted.

Rank is lower for a moment that stays. `networkBlackout` is 0. `bossPhaseBreak` and `lockdown` are 1. `captainTelegraph`, `extraction`, and `cameraDestroyed` are 2. `playerHit` and `exposureThreshold` are 3. `cameraAcquire` and `heatReinforcements` are 4. `enemyDefeat` and `enemyAlerted` are 5. `ghostStep` and `ricochet` are 6. `enemyHit` is 7. Any other id is 9.

The lifetime in frames is `max(1, (milliseconds * 60 + 999) / 1000)`. `expiresFrame` is the admit frame plus that count. `render` adds 1 to the unfrozen frame, then removes every instance whose expiry is at or before the new frame. A hit-stop frame does not take that step.

This does not change a cap.
