# Fog drift

Read 2026-11-04 from `FogDensity` and `WorldRenderer.renderFog` on `d873e91`. No fog percent was changed.

The day's percent stays in [daily-flavour-2026-11-04.md](daily-flavour-2026-11-04.md). The fight thinning stays in [fog-thinning-2026-11-04.md](fog-thinning-2026-11-04.md). The tile stays in [tiled-planes-2026-11-02.md](tiled-planes-2026-11-02.md).

`beginDailyRun` sets `fogDensity` to `fogPercent / 100`. Each frame multiplies that by the thinning opacity. `split` clamps the product at 0. The layer alpha is that value capped at 1. The excess above 1 is the boost. Each fog tile carries a second copy of itself, named `fog-density-boost`, and that copy's alpha is the excess.

Reduced motion holds both layers at offset 0. Otherwise the offset is `(tick % 1_000_000) * speed / 1000`, then wrapped by the tile. The low layer's speed is 120. The high layer's speed is 300. The layer position is that offset in x and half of it in y.

This does not change a fog percent.
