# Q8 scale

Read 2026-11-04 from `Q8` on `fcab4e5`. No scale was changed. The per-second conversion stays in [standard-enemy-stats-2026-11-04.md](standard-enemy-stats-2026-11-04.md).

One world unit is 256 raw. `unitsTruncated` divides the raw value by 256 and truncates toward zero. `VecQ8.contains` treats the boundary as inside: the squared distance is at most the squared radius.

Half-away division rounds a remainder of at least half the divisor away from zero. The 128-bit product form does the same for `(a * b) / d`.

This does not change a quantity.
