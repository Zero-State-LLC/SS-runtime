# HUD fit

Read 2026-11-04 from `HUDLayout` and `HUDProjector` on `e4ff026`. No authored rect was moved.

The reference rectangles stay in [control-rects-2026-11-04.md](control-rects-2026-11-04.md). The scale settings stay in [presentation-settings-2026-11-04.md](presentation-settings-2026-11-04.md). Half-away division stays in [q8-scale-2026-11-04.md](q8-scale-2026-11-04.md).

The canvas permille is the minimum of two half-away divisions: the safe width times 1000 over 844, and the safe height times 1000 over 390. The reference canvas is scaled by that permille and centred in the safe rectangle. An informational element then multiplies by the HUD scale, again half-away. A control maps at the standard scale. Its floor on each side is the greater of the authored side and 44. A mapped side under that floor grows about the control's own centre. The result is then kept inside the safe rectangle.

The projector's points per scene unit are the minimum of the view size over the scene size, or 1 when that fit is not positive. The safe width and height are the inset view, rounded down. A safe-rectangle point becomes scene units by subtracting the safe centre and dividing. Scene y is the negation of that point y.

This does not move an authored rect.
