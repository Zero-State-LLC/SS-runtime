`minX` is `center.x - halfSize.x`, and the other three edges are the matching sum or difference. `contains` of an integer point is true when each coordinate is at least the minimum and at most the maximum. `contains` of a Q8 point compares the raw value with `Int64(edge) * Q8.scale` on those same four sides.

`Bounds.aabb` sets the center to `(min + max) / 2` and the half-size to `(max - min) / 2`, on each axis.

This does not move an edge.
