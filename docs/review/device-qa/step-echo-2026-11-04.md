`ghostStep` adds an empty node on the world effect layer. Its trail count starts at the variant's particle count, and at least 1. An echo is considered every 3 unfrozen frames.

The echo is the player's silhouette at the player's current point. The stroke is red 0.55, green 0.85, blue 0.95, alpha 0.7, at line width 1.5. Reduced motion or reduced flash, read on that frame, draws one echo with a clear fill and then stops the trail. Otherwise the fill is that colour at alpha 0.18, the echo fades over 0.25 seconds, and the remaining count drops by 1.

This does not change a dodge length.
