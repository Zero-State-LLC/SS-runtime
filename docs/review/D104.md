### BP-004 playback reconciliation (2026-10-14)

Camera playback now keys its lifetime to authoritative Integrity and Reduced Motion,
rather than the one-tick event clip. A critical hit selects critical-entry immediately;
a later dormant snapshot no longer cancels the destruction sequence. Newer Integrity
cancels the old sequence, which settles on the appropriate terminal frame. Reduced
Motion cancels running actions and shows that terminal texture immediately. Field
visibility still reads the authoritative projection, independently of animation.

Four AppTests exercise the real renderer: critical selection despite a transient hit
clip, destruction persistence and immediate field removal, Reduced Motion terminal
textures for all integrity values, and interruption/node reuse across 3→2→1→0.
Local core suite: 760 tests / 107 suites passed (camera-playback-core.log in the task
workspace). Local iOS build-for-testing did not reach compilation: package manifest
cache access was denied; CoreSimulatorService was also inaccessible. App tests require
CI evidence before this change is considered validated.

This closes only playback wiring. Legacy head/housing composition and old art remain
pending the complete-device sheet admission. A terminal texture from historical art
is not proof of the required damaged/critical drawing, non-color markers or benchmark
fidelity. No art/catalog/metadata/gameplay identity changed.
