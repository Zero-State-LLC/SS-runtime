What the trial establishes

Midpoint is the selected ground basis, not a claim of faithful whole-scene reconstruction. At unchanged world focus, the fitted-reference mapping clips the quiet-scene building at all three zooms. Midpoint contains its canvas at 1× and 1.25×, but clips it at 1.4×. The baked building view remains unchanged rather than being warped to hide the mismatch.

Both mappings retain quiet-scene luminance and chroma drift at every zoom. The legal encounter has no palette flags except midpoint at 1.4×, which exceeds the chroma band. Player source-alpha height is approximately 15.3%, 19.1% and 21.4% of viewport height across the zooms, not a claim of visible unoccluded anatomy.

Limits retained after the choice

  • Quiet street is a controlled composition. Legal encounter uses the existing seed-1 Ghost Step world-command stream at tick 4976, not a newly staged combat fixture.
  • Existing baked building perspective and Player facings are unchanged. Mismatch with the fitted ground is visible evidence, not fixed by squashing or mirroring source art.
  • Projected roots, collision geometry, fields and projectile origins are measured separately from sprite bounds. Geometry does not come from alpha silhouettes.
  • The HUD stays unprojected. Nearer framing may crop actors or attacks. Canvas occupancy is not visible unoccluded anatomy.
  • Static captures and inverse-mapping tests do not establish live eight-direction facing, depth behavior, dense-play readability, physical performance or asset admission.
  • The approved palette reference and BP-011 review bands remain unchanged. See the report for measured differences and crop conventions.
